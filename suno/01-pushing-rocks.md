# 01 — Pushing Rocks

Where it started. West Oakland, the trade, and none of the romance.

```text
[Style: cold aggressive street rap, spoken bar delivery, no melody]
[Vocal: dry close-mic rap, percussive, syllable-heavy, hard consonants, no singing]

[Intro - spoken, dry, 808 only]
They romance this part. I'm not gonna.
West Oakland.
Trunk talk.

[Verse 1 - strict bars, no melody]
Cold in the Bottoms when the wind come off the water
Corner store light buzzing like it's counting up my hours
Sixteen and I'm learning what a scale really weigh
Ain't the product on the metal, it's the years it take away
Eight-eighty overhead sound like a river made of tires
Every headlight a maybe, every siren was a liar
Auntie on the porch ain't say nothing, but she knew
Whole block knew — and that's the thing that made it true
Fiends called me by my first name, that's the part they don't show
I knew they children birthdays and I served 'em all the same though
Told myself supply ain't the same thing as demand
That's a coward's arithmetic, I ran it in my hand
Man in the mirror had a jaw like a lockbox
Wouldn't open for the truth 'til the truth started knocking
Bought a chain, bought a car, ain't buy a single day
Everything I ever purchased came with interest I ain't pay

[Hook - chanted, spoken rhythm, NOT sung]
Pushing rocks off a block where the fog don't lift
Every dollar in my pocket had a hole up in it
Money came fast and it left the same way
Told the mirror I'd be gone by the end of May

[Verse 2 - strict bars, heavier emphasis, no melody]
Winter came and took my closest like a lease expired
Poured it out onto the curb, watched it run into the wire
Momma said "you next" — not a threat, just a forecast
Weatherman don't lie, he just tell you what the storm has
Kept it in the box spring, ain't never sleep flat
Rich up in a chain but I'm broke in a Laundromat
Counting on the dryer, hoodie up, hands shaking
That ain't cold — that's a body that a lifestyle taking
They dress the corner up in a song, I ain't gon' do it
It's a job with no HR and a boss that's ruthless
Clock in, clock out, only the clock don't stop
Every rock I ever pushed pushed back twice as hard
Left with a duffle, left with a scar, left with a name
Left before the block could put my picture in a frame
That's the whole receipt, I ain't dressing it up
Last time I speak on it — now watch me switch it up

[Bridge - spoken, half-time, 808 only, no melody]
Count the ones that made it out — I need one hand
Count the ones still standing there — I need a whole van
Same corner, same clock, same fog, same rain
Different faces every year and it don't ever change
They call it a game 'cause a game supposed to have a winner
I ain't never met one — I just met survivors
So don't put this on a poster, don't you make it look sweet
This a hole in the ground with a rhythm underneath

[Hook - chanted, spoken rhythm, NOT sung]
Pushing rocks off a block where the fog don't lift
Every dollar in my pocket had a hole up in it
Money came fast and it left the same way
Told the mirror I'd be gone by the end of May

[Outro - 808 fade, spoken tag]
End of May.
I meant it.
Trunk talk.
```
